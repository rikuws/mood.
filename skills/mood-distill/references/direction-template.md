# Distilled direction template

Write the brief in this order. Omit a section only when the board gives no evidence for it. Do not invent references, colors, typefaces, or constraints that are not visible in the saved items.

## Project

- Name, UUID, and item count from `pinax-agent`
- One line on what the collection actually contains (interiors, garments, interfaces, landscapes, objects, type, mixed)

## Thesis

Two to four sentences. State the atmosphere and intended creative direction as a whole, not a list of adjectives.

## Atmosphere

Temperature, energy, era, and density. Prefer concrete observations ("warm stone under hard daylight") over marketing language ("premium, luxurious, cohesive").

## Palette

Colors as they appear in the imagery, with a role each: ground, ink, accent, material, light. Quote approximate hex when you can see a dominant color; otherwise describe the color in words. Note when the board is nearly monochrome.

## Materials and texture

Surfaces, finishes, grain, paper, fabric, metal, glass, vegetation, or digital material that recur.

## Type and graphic language

Lettering, grid, ornament, photography style, UI chrome, or editorial devices that actually show up. Do not prescribe SF Pro, Inter, or a generic design-system type ramp unless those faces are in the board.

## Composition and space

Cropping, margin, symmetry, clutter vs emptiness, camera distance, stacking, and rhythm. For spatial boards: furniture scale, light direction, and how objects sit in a room. For interface boards: density, alignment, and how imagery meets chrome.

## Motifs

Recurring subjects, shapes, or gestures. Mention outliers only when they change the reading of the board.

## Voice

Tone taken from titles, notes, and quoted text. Preserve distinctive phrasing; do not rewrite someone else's sentence as if it were yours.

## Do / Don't

Short constraints an implementer can follow. Derive them from the board (what it consistently does and avoids), not from generic taste rules.

## Provenance

The handful of items that most strongly set the direction. For each: title, author or handle if present, source (`x` or `web`), and URL. These remain citations, not decoration to strip.

## Reusable prompt

One dense paragraph an agent can prepend to later work. It must be specific to this board. No filler such as "create a stunning modern design."
